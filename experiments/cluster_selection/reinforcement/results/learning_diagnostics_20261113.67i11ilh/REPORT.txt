exact resumptions of optimizer, RNG, teacher-cache or replay state.

Common evaluation limits and practical limits

All full comparisons use MRV/maximum-degree/name branching and allowance32,
8192 decoder/heuristic steps, maximum64 original cores and6 open variables,
per-cluster work1e6 and size1e5, with no cumulative tensor-work limit. The policy
chooses a cluster, contraction/GAC updates domains, and subsequent choices use
those domains. Summary cores remain temporary; pruning is retained.
Equal caps do not imply equal actual contractions or tensor work. For example,
the initial fresh comparison used estimated work 0.274m for the heuristic,
3.743m for random initialization, 0.557m for the teacher and 7.650m for REINFORCE.
Concurrent CPU timings are descriptive, not isolated speed comparisons.
Old eight-state validation of the same frozen chunk52 weights starts at16 nodes
in the current local runtime, versus11 historically. The current matched control
is used throughout. Torch/runtime differences are a possible unisolated cause;
historical and current counts must not be combined as a learning trajectory.
